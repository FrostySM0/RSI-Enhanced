Before you enable RSI Enhanced

RSI Enhanced reads relevant RSI pages to apply your appearance settings and the optional tools you choose to use.

It stores settings, saved Spectrum thread links and titles, hidden-user handles and names, Hangar favourites, view choices and search details, and cached public funding figures in this browser.

Some tools make read-only requests to RSI using your existing sign-in session. The extension does not read or store your password or session cookies. Funding data comes from a public Google Sheet; no RSI account or Hangar data is sent to Google.

Your saved extension data is not sent to the developer. RSI Enhanced has no analytics or telemetry. You can remove locally saved data by clearing the extension’s stored data in your browser.
