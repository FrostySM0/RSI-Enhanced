Quick setup

Get started in three steps

Choose your browser’s store, then enable only the RSI Enhanced features you want to use.

  1. 01

    Install from your browser store

    The Chrome Web Store is available for Chrome, Edge and Brave; Firefox is available from Add-ons.

  2. 02

    Open the RSI website

    Visit Spectrum or another supported RSI page in your browser.

  3. 03

    Choose the features you want

    Open RSI Enhanced settings and switch on the tools and appearance options that suit you.

What’s new in RSI Enhanced 1.2.0View release notes

Version 1.2.0 adds new Hangar tools, makes ship research easier, and brings more customization to RSI and Spectrum.

ADDED

  • My Hangar now supports saved views, Collections, and a change report after you manually refresh your Hangar.
  • Compare related ships and vehicles, see what differs, and check which variants you own when Hangar data is available.
  • Search and filter Buy Back pledges by name, item type, or reclaim month.
  • The Verse Data Hub searches public ships, vehicles, components, weapons, gear, locations, missions, and lore from supported RSI pages.
  • Spectrum Advanced Search can save named searches for reuse.
  • The Blocked Users page has per-member Hide posts controls, separate from RSI's Unblock action.
  • A one-time update notice links to the release notes after RSI Enhanced updates.
  • The global account panel lets you switch between RSI's appearance and RSI Enhanced.
  • My Gear offers an optional Flight Deck page skin.

CHANGED

  • Extension Settings is now a full-page Appearance Studio with searchable themes and fonts, live previews, and feature controls.
  • Spectrum Settings navigation now uses grouped sections and a gold marker for the current page.
  • The theme gallery adds more than 40 styles and color palettes, including gradient themes and Terminal Green.
  • Spectrum Friends is now a full-width directory, and friend avatars follow your avatar frame setting.
  • Spectrum member profiles have a redesigned card and can show the public Enlisted date when available.
  • The Spectrum reply composer follows your selected theme and has a cleaner layout while keeping its formatting and publishing controls.
  • The Pledge Store cart shows the Cart, Address, and Payment checkout steps.

FIXED

  • Spectrum's Settings, organization rows, Friends and Blocked Users tables, Bookmarks, Search, and top-bar controls now follow your selected theme instead of retaining cyan surfaces.
  • The Firefox extension now installs with its supported background script setup.
Privacy, in plain language

Your saved choices stay in your browser.

Settings and saved feature data stay in your browser. RSI Enhanced has no analytics or telemetry and does not send saved data to the developer. Optional tools may request RSI page data, public ship prices from the SC Wiki API, or funding history from Google Sheets when you use them. Read the full policy for details.
