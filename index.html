Quick setup

Get started in three steps

Choose your browser’s store, then enable only the RSI Enhanced features you want to use.

  1. 01

    Install from your browser store

    Use the Chrome Web Store today; the Firefox listing is coming soon.

  2. 02

    Open the RSI website

    Visit Spectrum or another supported RSI page in your browser.

  3. 03

    Choose the features you want

    Open RSI Enhanced settings and switch on the tools and appearance options that suit you.

Privacy, in plain language

Your saved choices stay in your browser.

Settings and saved feature data are stored locally by the extension. RSI Enhanced has no analytics or telemetry and does not send your saved data to the developer. Some optional features request page or reply data from RSI, and Funding Goals reads a public spreadsheet. Read the full policy for details.
